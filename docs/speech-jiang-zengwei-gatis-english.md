# Address at the China Tourism Group Duty Free Corporation 2026 Global Partners Conference

**Jiang Zengwei, Chairman of the Global Alliance for Trade in Services (GATIS)**

**March 27, 2026**

---

Distinguished ______,

Dear guests and friends,

Good day to all!

It is a great pleasure and honor to be invited to the China Duty Free Group 2026 Global Partners Conference. First, on behalf of the Global Alliance for Trade in Services, I would like to extend my warmest congratulations on the grand opening of this conference, and my sincere regards to all the distinguished guests who have long been dedicated to the tourism retail industry and to advancing innovation in travel services trade!

We are living in an era of profound transformation. Geopolitical conflicts have compounded the twists and turns of the global economic recovery, and unilateralism has disrupted the multilateral international order established in the post-war era. Nevertheless, the historical tide of technological innovation, peaceful development, and win-win cooperation is unstoppable. Today, the fact that all of you have gathered here to discuss collaboration, pursue shared development, and envision the future together not only demonstrates the tremendous influence and brand appeal of China Duty Free Group as an industry leader, but also sends a clear and resolute message: openness and cooperation are the irreversible trend of our times, and joining hands for mutual benefit is our collective choice in the face of challenges.

In recent years, global services trade has maintained rapid and sustained growth, becoming a vital force driving world economic growth. Fueled by the booming demand in travel services trade, tourism-related distribution and retail — particularly the duty-free market — are embracing new development opportunities. As an important component of international services trade, duty-free distribution serves as a critical nexus linking production with consumption and connecting domestic markets with international ones, playing a unique role in the international cultural trade system of mutual learning and in the open and cooperative international supply chain system.

It is encouraging that the development of China's domestic duty-free industry has been remarkable for all to see. Driven by policy support, consumption upgrading, and business model innovation, the Chinese duty-free market has become not only a major engine of growth for the global duty-free industry, but also a powerful catalyst for the recovery of global services trade. We are delighted to see that China Duty Free Group has seized opportunities with an open posture, deepened cooperation through pragmatic measures, and ridden the wave of expanding domestic demand and boosting consumption. Currently, China Duty Free Group has established long-term and stable partnerships with over 1,500 renowned global brands. Whether in product innovation, brand revitalization, or experiential retail; whether in international cooperation, industrial integration, or service quality enhancement — the Group has been striving for excellence in every dimension. It continues to lead in Hainan offshore duty-free, port-of-entry duty-free, downtown duty-free, and digital retail operations, growing into a world-class travel consumer goods retail operator. These figures and achievements are a vivid reflection of the resilience, potential, vitality, and positive long-term trajectory of the Chinese economy, and the best testament to the opportunity for global tourism consumption to share in China's growth.

Ladies and gentlemen!

The Global Alliance for Trade in Services, an international non-governmental organization personally initiated and supported by President Xi Jinping and jointly founded by services trade enterprises and institutions from around the world, has always upheld the principles of "neutrality, openness, inclusiveness, and innovation." It is committed to building three core functions — "cooperation and exchange, think tank research, and policy dialogue" — and actively serves as a bridge and link, working hand in hand with enterprises, institutions, and professionals to jointly promote the continued advancement and innovative development of tourism retail and services trade. Taking this opportunity, I would like to share three perspectives drawing on the work of the Global Alliance for Trade in Services.
